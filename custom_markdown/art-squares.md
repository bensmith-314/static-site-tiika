# Art Squares
As a way of commemorating progress in the [everyday project](/everyday), I am creating a collage of the art pieces for every *square* day (1, 4, 9, 16, etc.). As much as I would love to post the multi-GB files that are up to 50,000px wide, I have bandwidth considerations, so each art square has been resized to 8192x8192 pixels with some compression for the more recent squares. Each square is made by sequentially stitching the daily art pieces together from left to right then from top to bottom. Enjoy!
